👤 External Person / Contact Submission Form

Simulate sending a Person / Contact via Webhook into CRM with Full Bearer Token Header

Local Server Endpoint: http://localhost:3040/api/persons or Vite Proxy: /api/persons
Click "Auto-Fetch Full Token" or manually paste your full Bearer <JWT_TOKEN> string.
Tip: Use a unique email address (e.g. jane.doe.101@techcorp.com) when testing multiple submissions.
